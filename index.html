About TrustTag

Our Mission

To create a safer digital ecosystem by enabling instant identity verification through secure QR technology. We aim to prevent fraud, protect vulnerable populations, and build trust between customers and service providers.

Our Vision

A world where every service interaction is secure, transparent, and verified. Where customers feel safe and service providers are valued for their authenticity.

Why TrustTag Was Created

  • Prevent identity-based fraud and impersonation
  • Reduce crimes involving fake delivery agents and unauthorized drivers
  • Improve public safety during service interactions
  • Support women's safety initiatives
  • Protect elderly citizens from scams
  • Create accountability and transparency in service industry

How TrustTag Works

1

Service Provider Registration

Individual service providers or organizations register on TrustTag with verified details, photo, and ID proof.

→
2

Identity Verification

Admin team verifies documents, background, and credentials for authenticity and safety compliance.

→
3

QR Code Generation

Unique QR code is generated containing verified identity details and security credentials.

→
4

Share Your QR Code

Service provider displays QR code on ID badge, vehicle, or delivery package for customer verification.

→
5

Customer Verification

Customer scans QR code to instantly view verified identity, photo, company name, and verification status.
